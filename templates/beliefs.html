CMRI Beliefs

The Congregation of Mary Immaculate Queen (CMRI) is a traditional Catholic religious congregation dedicated to preserving and practicing the Roman Catholic faith as it has been handed down through the centuries. Our mission is to remain faithful to the teachings, worship, and traditions of the Catholic Church.

What We Believe

The Traditional Catholic Faith

We profess all the doctrines taught by the Catholic Church throughout the ages and hold firmly to Sacred Scripture, Sacred Tradition, and the perennial teachings of the Church.

Please note that we do not follow Vatican II because we believe it departed from traditional Catholic teachings and practices.

The Holy Sacrifice of the Mass

We celebrate the traditional Latin Mass according to the Roman Missal as it existed before the liturgical reforms following the Second Vatican Council. We believe the Mass is the unbloody renewal of Christ's Sacrifice on Calvary.

The Seven Sacraments

We believe in and administer the seven sacraments instituted by Our Lord Jesus Christ:

  • Baptism
  • Confirmation
  • Holy Communion
  • Confession
  • Extreme Unction (Anointing)
  • Holy Orders
  • Matrimony

Devotion to the Blessed Virgin Mary

As children of Mary Immaculate, we foster a deep devotion to the Blessed Virgin Mary, honoring her as the Mother of God and Queen of Heaven. We encourage the daily recitation of the Holy Rosary and devotion to her Immaculate Heart.

The Communion of Saints

We honor the saints as heroic examples of Christian virtue and seek their intercession before God. We also pray for the souls in Purgatory.

The Sede Vacante Position

CMRI holds the position that the Holy See is presently vacant (sede vacante). While recognizing the divine institution of the papacy and its essential role in the Church, we believe that the crisis in the Church following the reforms of the 1960s has resulted in a vacancy of the Apostolic See. This position is held in an effort to preserve the integrity of the Catholic Faith as it has always been taught.

Our Mission

Our mission is to know, love, and serve God; to preserve the traditional Catholic Faith; to sanctify souls through the sacraments; and to promote devotion to Our Lord Jesus Christ and His Blessed Mother, Mary Immaculate Queen.

"Our Lady of Mount Carmel, Star of the Sea, guide us safely to Christ."
